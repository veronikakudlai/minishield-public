### 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the MiniShield application for Safari (the “App”) handles information, how its extensions work, how preferences are stored locally, and how protection rules are updated.

MiniShield helps reduce ads, tracking requests, cookie notices and certain intrusive interface elements in Safari. The App does not require registration, a user account or sending the developer a list of websites you visit in order to provide its core functions.

### 2. Information used on your device

To apply the protection options you select, the App may store the following on your device:

- whether protection is enabled, the selected mode and individual feature preferences;
- full-site exclusions, cookie-only exclusions and site-compatibility exclusions (which disable native CSS hiding);
- locally stored rules, their versions, checksums and information used to validate updates and restore a previous version;
- technical state indicating whether updated settings have been applied to the Safari extension.

Within permissions granted in Safari, the Web Extension may access information from an open page to identify ad elements, cookie notices and optional sign-in prompts. This may include the page address, DOM structure, element attributes, visible control labels and limited indicators of cookie state. These details are processed locally and are not sent to the developer.

### 3. Where information is stored

Preferences, exclusions and installed rules are stored locally in an App Group container shared by the App and its extensions. MiniShield does not require a cloud account to provide its core functions. The current version does not synchronize this information through a developer-operated server.

Information used to recognize page elements is handled in the Safari page context. The App does not maintain a centralized browsing history or a record of every page viewed.

If device backup is enabled, certain locally stored information may be included in a backup according to your operating-system settings and the terms of your chosen service. MiniShield does not manage those backups.

### 4. Purposes of processing

Locally stored information is used to provide the features you choose: network filtering, handling page elements, removing recognized tracking parameters from links, applying exclusions, validating rules, downloading updates and restoring earlier rule versions.

MiniShield does not include advertising analytics SDKs, create a browsing profile for the developer, sell browsing history or use website information for personalized advertising.

### 5. Cookie notices and actions on webpages

MiniShield may apply Safari network rules that prevent certain known cookie-notice scripts from loading. If a notice appears, the extension first tries to hide it safely. **If hiding succeeds, it does not perform an automatic cookie-rejection action for that notice**: only the interface visibility changes.

If the notice remains visible, supported situations and enabled settings may permit MiniShield to attempt rejection of optional cookies and verify the result. This does not guarantee that the website recorded the user's choice. Hiding a notice does not, by itself, establish cookie rejection or that tracking has stopped.

MiniShield is not a VPN, proxy or anonymity service. It works within Safari and does not filter traffic from other applications.

### 6. Permissions, network connections and third parties

To provide on-page features, the Safari Web Extension requests access to websites you authorize, communicates with the containing App, and uses current-tab functionality. Safari controls these permissions, including access in private browsing.

When checking for and downloading rule updates, MiniShield establishes HTTPS connections to external file-hosting services, including GitHub services. Those services may receive ordinary connection information, such as IP address, request time and technical HTTP connection data, under its own terms and privacy practices. Update requests do not include browsing history, webpage text or your cookie-consent decisions.

App installation and updates, Safari operation and device backups may also involve independent processing by the operating-system provider, app marketplace or a service of your choosing. This Policy does not govern those independent services.

### 7. Retention, choices and deletion

You can change protection modes, turn features off, manage website exclusions and disable Safari extensions through Safari settings. Local rule versions and recovery snapshots remain until replaced or removed through App functionality.

Removing the App from your device will generally remove its local app data as managed by iOS. Device backups and data held by independent services may remain and must be managed through those services or the relevant device settings.

Because the developer does not receive a record of the websites you visit or the content of those pages, the developer does not maintain a remote copy of that information to export or delete on request.

### 8. Information security and limitations

MiniShield aims to limit information handling to what is necessary for its features. Information is stored within the device's operating-system environment. Externally downloaded rule updates are verified using cryptographic signatures and checksums before being applied.

No mechanism can provide absolute protection from all ads or tracking technologies. We recommend keeping your device and operating system updated and using available security features such as a device passcode.

### 9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy if MiniShield's features, information practices or applicable requirements change. The current version will be available at the official public link listed in the App and on its app-store listing. Where required by applicable law or platform rules, appropriate notice will be provided about material changes.
